push_swap

C · algorithms · 42 · source

Sort a list of integers using two stacks, A and B, and only these operations: swap the top two (sa sb ss), push the top element across (pa pb), rotate up (ra rb rr) or rotate down (rra rrb rrr). The goal is the shortest sequence of operations.

How it works

The input is checked (integers only, no duplicates, no overflow) and normalised to ranks 0…n-1. Small inputs use hard-coded sorts; larger ones are sorted by the main strategy described in the README.

Typical results: about 590 operations for 100 numbers and 5200 for 500.
